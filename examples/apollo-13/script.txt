April thirteenth, nineteen seventy. Three astronauts are two hundred thousand miles from Earth, on their way to the Moon. Then... a bang. An oxygen tank has exploded. Their ship is leaking oxygen into space. "Houston, we've had a problem." The Moon landing is over. Now there is one goal: get home alive. They move into the lunar lander, built to keep two men alive for two days. It has to keep three alive for four. To save power, they shut almost everything down. The cabin drops to just above freezing. Then their own breath turns the air toxic. The spare air filters are square. The holes are round. So Houston builds a fix from things on board: a flight-plan cover, bags, duct tape... and a sock. It works. They swing around the Moon, farther from Earth than anyone had ever been, and fall back home. April seventeenth. Three parachutes over the Pacific. All three came home.
