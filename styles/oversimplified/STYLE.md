Look (LOOKED, from the 1 fps sheets):
- Countries are characters: the real map silhouette, black stick arms and legs, a one-line mouth; big white eyes pop on for reactions.
- Generic people: white tube bodies with a round head, ". ." or "> <" eyes, a curved "(" arm line. Named people: thin black stick figures (he uses photo heads; we never use real faces).
- Speech: lowercase hand lettering, no balloon, a short tick toward the speaker. Labels: bold condensed caps with a tick. Big moments: white or red condensed caps (Impact-like). Years: big red type.
- Maps: flat bright land, white borders, a dark textured sea. Backgrounds: flat cartoon scenes, a white page, or a light grey radial vignette. A title sign swings in on ropes.
- Motion blur appears only on whip pans; we use hard cuts instead (no blur, the director's rule).

What this means against the Kurzgesagt rules: hard cuts are correct here, holds are correct here (the camera may park), text pops instead of fading. Measure the reference before reusing any rule from another style.
